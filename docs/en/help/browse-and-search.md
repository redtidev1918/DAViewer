# Browsing and searching

**Language / 语言:** [中文](/help/browse-and-search.md) · English

## Search

Tap the **Search** tab in the bottom navigation.

- Search for **artworks** or **users**: type a keyword and switch type with the "Artworks / Users" toggle under the search box.
- Search a **tag**: type `#tagname` (e.g. `#digitalpainting`) and press enter to open the tag page. Tag pages sort by Default / New / Popular and list related tags to jump to.
- Artwork results sort by Default / Newest (menu in the top bar).
- With no query you'll see recent searches (removable one by one or all at once), recommended tags, and popular tags.

## Long-press menu

**Long-press any artwork card** in a list to get:

- **Hide this artwork** / **Hide artworks by this artist**: removed from your lists; undo anytime in [Content filters](/en/content-filtering.md).
- **View details**: open the artwork page.
- **Favourite / Unfavourite**.
- **Share**, **Copy link**, **Open in browser**.

## Favourites

- Tap the **favourite** (heart) button on an artwork page, or the quick button on any card.
- Everything you've favourited is under the **Favourites** tab.
- Favourites require sign-in — they're stored in your DeviantArt account.

## Browsing history

Artworks you view are recorded locally. Open **Settings → Browsing history**; "Clear" wipes it. History never leaves this device.

## Opening a link

Got a DeviantArt link from a friend? Tap the link icon in the home screen's top bar (**Open link**), paste the URL (e.g. `https://www.deviantart.com/xxx/art/xxx-123456789`), and open it inside the app.

## Notifications

The bell in the home top bar is your **notification** center (favourites, comments, and other DeviantArt messages). "Mark all as read" is at the top.
