# Downloading artworks

**Language / 语言:** [中文](/help/download-artwork.md) · English

## How do I download?

Open an artwork's detail page and look at the "Original:" status in the download section:

| Status | Meaning | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Downloadable | The original file can be downloaded | Tap **Download original** |
| Sign-in required | The website session expired | Sign in again — see [Login and account](login-and-account.md) |
| Purchase required | It's a paid work | Buy it on deviantart.com first |
| Restricted | Your account can't access it (e.g. mature content) | See the mature-content entry in the [FAQ](faq.md) |
| Not downloadable | The artist disabled downloads | Use "save current quality" below |
| Deleted or missing | The work was taken down | Can't download |

- When the original isn't downloadable, the app offers to **save the highest-quality image currently shown** instead.
- While downloading you can **Pause / Resume / Cancel**.
- **Multi-page works** (cards marked "MULTI"): downloading the original queues every page automatically.
- If the status looks wrong, tap "Re-check download permission" and try again.

## Managing downloads

The **Downloads** tab in the bottom navigation manages all tasks:

- Each task shows the filename, state (Completed / Downloading / Paused / Failed / Source expired / Cancelled / Queued), and progress.
- Tap **Open** to view the image in-app; on desktop there's also **Open folder** to reveal the file.
- **Delete finished** in the top bar clears completed records and their local files (with a confirmation first).

## Where are the files?

Completed downloads are moved to your **system's public Downloads folder** — the same one you see in Finder or your file manager.

- On Android, "Stored in the app" means the system didn't grant the public folder; the file lives in the app's private storage and is reachable via the system Files app or a USB connection.
- Failed or paused tasks stay in the app and resume later.

## Downloads keep failing

1. Check your connection in [Networking and proxy](network.md).
2. "Source expired" usually means the work changed or was taken down — reopen it from its page.
3. Persistent failures: export logs in **Settings → Diagnostics** and report the problem.
